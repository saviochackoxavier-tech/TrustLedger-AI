# Privacy Policy

**TrustLedger AI — Autonomous Vendor Escrow & Dispute Mediator**

Version 1.0 · Effective date: 2026-10-30

> **Deployment scope.** This policy describes the reference implementation
> submitted for the PayPal AI Hackathon 2026 (PayPal Sandbox build). A
> production operator must publish it at a public URL with real contact data.

---

## 1. Who we are

The operator of TrustLedger AI ("we", "us") acts as the data controller for
the personal data described below. Contact: privacy@trustledger.example
(replace before deployment).

## 2. Data we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account / party metadata | Client and vendor references (PayPal payer/payee IDs or email addresses) | Entered by you |
| Transaction metadata | Milestone title, description, amount, currency, PayPal order/capture/payout IDs, settlement status | Generated by the Service |
| Dispute evidence | Chat logs, delivery notes, contract excerpts you submit | Entered by you |
| Delivery proof | Artifact URLs, commit references, delivery notes | Entered by you |
| AI outputs | Verdict, confidence score, reasoning | Google Gemini (processor) |
| Technical logs | HTTP request logs, error logs, audit-ledger entries | Automatic |

We do **not** collect special-category data, and the Service has no
authentication system in this build (no passwords are stored).

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

- **Performing the escrow agreement** (contractual necessity): party
  metadata, transaction metadata, delivery proof.
- **Dispute resolution** (contractual necessity / legitimate interest):
  evidence logs, AI outputs.
- **Fraud prevention, security and audit integrity** (legitimate interest /
  legal obligation): technical logs, hash-chained audit ledger.
- **Non-identified analytics** (legitimate interest): aggregate usage counts.

## 4. Processors and transfers

- **PayPal (PayPal Holdings, Inc., US)** — payment capture and payout
  execution under PayPal's developer and merchant agreements.
- **Google (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC)** — Gemini arbitration and
  delivery audits. See `AI_DATA_USAGE.md`.
- **Hosting provider** — application and database hosting (SQLite in this
  build; a production deployment uses a managed database).

Some recipients are located outside the EEA/UK; transfers rely on the
appropriate safeguard for the recipient (e.g., EU Standard Contractual
Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework), as documented by each processor.

## 5. Retention

- **Milestone, transaction and audit-ledger records**: retained for the
  statutory bookkeeping period (commonly 7–10 years depending on
  jurisdiction) because escrow records are financial records.
- **Dispute evidence and AI reasoning**: retained for 24 months after
  settlement, unless a legal hold applies.
- **Technical logs**: 90 days.
- Hash-chained audit entries are designed for integrity and may be retained
  in append-only storage; deletion requests are honored for the underlying
  personal data where legally permissible (see §6).

## 6. Your rights

Where GDPR (EU) or CCPA/CPRA (California) applies, you may request: access,
rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection; California
residents may opt out of "sales/sharing" (we do not sell personal data). We
respond within statutory timeframes. You may lodge a complaint with your
supervisory authority. Some financial records cannot be erased before the end
of the retention period; in that case we restrict processing instead.

## 7. Security

See `DATA_PROTECTION.md` for the technical and organizational safeguards,
including tamper-evident hashing, strict input sanitization, CSRF protection,
and fail-closed money movement.

## 8. Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18.

## 9. Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date when practices change.
